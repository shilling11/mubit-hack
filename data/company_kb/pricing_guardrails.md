# Ashgrove Pricing Guardrails (fictional, internal)

## Fixed-price rules

Fixed prices are offered only for a defined, capped scope: named use cases, channels and volume bands. Additional use cases or channels are priced through change control using the rate card. Ashgrove never offers a fixed price for unlimited or uncapped scope.

## Outcome-based pricing

No guaranteed benefit commitments (for example handling-time or cost reductions) are made before a discovery baseline is agreed. After the baseline, at most 10% of fees may be placed at risk against agreed outcomes, with Commercial Director approval.

## Volume bands and assumptions

Platform subscription is priced per named agent in bands (up to 250, 251–1,000, 1,000+). Usage charges are per 1,000 AI interactions, with assumptions referenced to the client's volumes tab.

## Rate card

Day rates for change requests: consultant GBP 950, solution architect GBP 1,250, engineer GBP 850.
