# Ashgrove Managed Service SLA Standard (fictional)

Owner: Managed Service Owner, Ashgrove Digital. Version 4.2.

## Availability commitment

Standard managed-service availability is 99.9% per calendar month for production services, excluding agreed planned maintenance windows. The Enhanced Resilience option (active-active across Azure UK South and UK West) raises the target to 99.95% for an uplift of 22% on the platform subscription. Ashgrove does not contract availability above 99.95%. Any availability commitment above the 99.9% standard requires written approval from the Managed Service Owner before it is included in a bid.

## Service credits

Service credits apply when monthly availability falls below the contracted target: 5% of the monthly service fee per 0.1 percentage point below target, capped at 15% of the monthly fee.

## Support hours and response times

Priority 1 incidents are supported 24x7 with a 30-minute response target. Priority 2: 2-hour response, 24x7. Priority 3: next business day. Faster response targets are not part of the standard service.

## Support delivery model

Ashgrove runs a follow-the-sun support model. The service desk and L2 engineering operate from Manchester (UK hours) and Bengaluru, India (UK overnight and weekend cover). Engineers in Bengaluru may access production environments remotely to resolve incidents, using just-in-time privileged access. Customer data is not stored outside the UK, but it can be viewed remotely by support staff during an incident.

## UK Sovereign Support option

For clients that require UK-only access to their data, the UK Sovereign Support option restricts all production access to UK-based, security-cleared staff. It carries an 18% uplift on the managed-service fee, and the overnight Priority 1 response target becomes 45 minutes.

## Recovery objectives

Standard recovery objectives: RTO 4 hours, RPO 15 minutes, with daily backups retained for 35 days and annual DR testing. The Enhanced Resilience option provides RTO 1 hour and RPO 5 minutes with twice-yearly failover tests.

## Monitoring and incident management

24x7 service monitoring, ITIL-aligned incident, problem and change management, monthly service reports and a named service delivery manager.
