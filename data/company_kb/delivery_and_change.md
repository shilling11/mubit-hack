# Ashgrove Delivery and Change Approach (fictional)

## Delivery roles and governance

Every engagement has a named delivery lead, solution architect, security lead and change lead, with weekly RAID reviews and fortnightly steering committees.

## Training and adoption

Role-based training (agents, team leaders, knowledge owners, administrators) delivered in person and through e-learning, with adoption measured through platform telemetry and pulse surveys.

## Knowledge transfer and runbooks

Operational runbooks, knowledge-transfer sessions and shadowing are completed and signed off before transition to service.
