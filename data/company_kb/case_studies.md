# Ashgrove Case Studies (fictional)

## Harlow and Finch retail contact centre

A fictional UK retailer deployed agent assist and summarisation for 400 agents. After a 12-week baseline, average handling time fell 12% and after-call work fell 35%.

## Brightwater Energy self-service

A fictional energy supplier launched authenticated web-chat self-service with human escalation. Containment rose 8 percentage points in six months.

## Clearpath Insurance claims triage

A fictional insurer used AI triage for claims notifications. Every settlement decision stayed with a human handler; triage time fell 40%.

## Westmoor Borough Council resident services

A fictional council launched a resident enquiries assistant with UK Sovereign Support and a human decision-maker for all eligibility outcomes.
